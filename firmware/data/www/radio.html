Captured startup signals

INIT GPIO2 and ATT GPIO4, SHIFT GPIO5, LATCH GPIO6 start high-impedance. Their meanings and safe electrical states are unverified. These controls are for an isolated, powered bench setup.

INIT (GPIO2): Unknown

LATCH (GPIO6): Unknown

Replay sends the captured LATCH pulse, unknown 96-clock burst, two latched 16-bit words and first 24-clock burst. It does not repeat the later 24-clock traffic; timing is approximate and measured from the button, not the INIT transition. Command meanings are unknown. Set INIT HIGH and both mutes LOW first.

Sony display

Uses all 22 matrices; titles longer than 22 characters scroll on the ESP. No browser is needed once enabled. Output never starts automatically after reboot.

Checking display...

Spectrum uses actual decoded audio: left 11 matrices are LEFT, right 11 are RIGHT, each bass to treble (20 Hz–20 kHz). On the Sony, MONO 1 labels LEFT and MONO 2 labels RIGHT; STEREO still indicates an actual two-channel source. Seven-row bars attack quickly and decay smoothly. It replaces the title on the Sony, not the sound: this is a visualization, not an audio equalizer.

All 26 slots stay scanned, even with one character. Do not divide brightness by the number of lit characters. Current limits are not measured.

STEREO / left MONO follow source channels. Bars show reported bitrate up to 320 kbps; dots mean buffering or unknown bitrate. TUNED means decoded playback. MUSIC / SPEECH use station genre, not song analysis.

Your own stream

HTTP/HTTPS MP3 or AAC (ADTS), 32 / 44.1 / 48 kHz. Not a website, playlist, HLS or Spotify link. The URL is saved; boot still starts NPO Radio 2.

Stream from your phone or Mac

Select this receiver in your app's AirPlay audio menu on the same Wi-Fi network. Radio stops while AirPlay is connected and resumes when you disconnect. Pausing AirPlay keeps the connection.

Bluetooth audio is not supported by the ESP32-S3.
