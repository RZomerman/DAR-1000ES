Runtime timing laboratory

0.3.26: radio and 22-character NOW PLAYING added. The AHCT v2 scan rendered clean text across all 22 matrices after STB was connected to BOTH TD chips. The working dim setting is 0.5% per grid: 39 us ON in each 300 us slot, 13% total CLEAR duty, about 128 Hz refresh. Count all 26 scanned slots, not just lit glyphs. Actual chip-side analogue levels and current remain unmeasured. Stop does not disable the translator.

New generated driver: blank, shift data with STB LOW, latch while blanked, then pulse CLEAR. No capture resampling. All settings apply with the current 26 editor words; no reflash is needed. Stop before switching between the legacy driver and this driver. Timing changes on the generated driver are queued atomically at DMA block boundaries (up to two blocks ahead).

Local held alternation switches the selected slot and slot minus one using the existing GPIO preload / CLEAR-only DMA path. Dwell is a scheduling target, not guaranteed physical timing. Each reload blanks and drains PWM first. Optional blank steps hold CLEAR LOW throughout; three steps give [first grid / second grid / blank / blank / blank], then repeat. Each step uses the selected dwell. Blank steps reduce average brightness and repetition rate; they do not increase the permitted ON duty. The displayed PWM duty applies while a grid is selected, not to its total time-averaged duty across alternation.

Pair mode alternates the selected slot and slot minus one. It reloads all 40 grid bits before every latch while blanked. This diagnostic is not yet verified on connected hardware; it is not the normal 26-slot scan. All six pins use GPIO, not DMA. Edge/ON/OFF times must be whole microseconds, and ON is limited to 50 us. Actual refresh is slower than the nominal estimate because of GPIO overhead and blanked task-yield gaps.

Scan: [blank / send / latch / ON / OFF] for each of 26 slots. Increase pulses per slot for [send once / ON / OFF / ON / OFF ...]. Held-grid: preload the selected grid and its word once, then only pulse CLEAR. DATA stays LOW, clocks HIGH and STB LOW as static GPIO, not DMA outputs. Only changing the selected slot or its word causes another preload; changing brightness does not resend data. Stop/start explicitly reloads the word. This tests ONE field, not a stored full screen. Other grids remain off.

Scan waveforms and CLEAR PWM have 125 ns resolution at 8 MHz. Held-mode preload uses GPIO delays rounded UP to whole 1 us, plus GPIO overhead; interrupts may extend them. Edge changes take effect at the next preload, not while holding a word. Stop/start to retest shift timings. Edge inputs: 250..20000 ns, in 125 ns steps. CK/STB minimum here is 250 ns (datasheet 70 ns); DATA setup/hold and CK-to-STB margins are at least 250 ns (datasheet 20/20/50 ns). Blank guards and CLEAR OFF are at least 2 us for output propagation. ON is capped at 280 us; pulses per slot 1..16. The datasheet's recommended operating limits are stricter: CK <= 8 MHz, CK width >= 75 ns, DATA setup/hold >= 50 ns; these software margins exceed them. Held-grid duty is capped at 1/26 (OFF >= 25 x ON), a conservative software guard, not a manufacturer glass rating. Frame duration is limited by the 65 ms DMA buffer. No setting is a guarantee of safe VFD drive.

Presets only change form values, never start output. Settings may be saved in this browser; firmware does not restore or start output on reboot. Analogue edge times (maximum 1 us), 5 V thresholds, translator glitches and visual flicker still require measurement at the TD62 inputs. Isolate the Sony signal wires for USB reset/reflash or when its logic supply is off: inputs must never exceed VDD + 0.5 V (absolute maximum). Idle clocks are HIGH even after Stop. Never probe VFD high voltage.

The legacy continuous mode scales captured slot timing for an approximately 250 Hz
refresh, scaling dim blanking proportionally. This deliberately differs from
the captured Sony timing and needs analyser validation. The first eight-frame DMA
chunk preloads registers while blanked; later chunks continue without repeating
the blank/preload sequence. A DMA alignment hold of under 8 us remains. Stop waits
for the current chunk (about 32 ms plus overhead), then holds CLEAR LOW.
This is not a hardware fail-safe against CPU/power failure. No output starts on boot
or merely by loading this page.

All previous audio and control assignments are disabled. No scan runs on boot.
An enabled translator can expose floating inputs during reset or while its GPIO
profile is inactive. Hardware-disable unused interfaces and provide external
biasing where required; software does not guarantee reset-time levels.


Disconnected analyser verification is recommended first. Driver clear/latch

Captured replay initializes both registers with grid CLEAR LOW, then sends
one 1.017-second blink cycle by DMA, including the recorded blank intervals.
Afterwards it holds CLEAR LOW, STB LOW and both clocks HIGH. Stop waits
for the DMA cycle to complete; it is not an emergency cutoff.
Initialization, scan words and clock handover have been checked at the
buffered outputs with an analyser. Correct live display operation is still
unverified. Stop returns to CLEAR LOW, STB LOW and both clocks HIGH.
