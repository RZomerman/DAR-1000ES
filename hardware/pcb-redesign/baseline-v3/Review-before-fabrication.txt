DAR-1000ES v3 - bottom routing and ESP power capacitors

Construction confirmed: bottom-layer tracks with normal PLATED through-hole
pads. Copper annular rings on both faces are intentional. This is NOT a
non-plated Sony phenolic-board fabrication file.

All routed copper must be B.Cu. No top-side tracks or routing vias are
permitted. Component-side insulated wire links are real assembly parts,
not copper tracks; install every link in Wire-links-v3.txt. A link with
more than two terminals joins ALL of its listed holes with insulated
wire; insulate any off-board wire junction. The assembly PDF shows the
links. The drawn bends are an assembly guide, not copper.

Wire links are board-only same-net routing components, like physical bus
links, using KiCad 10's explicit internal-jumper connectivity property.
They do not change the logical schematic. Do not remove them as spares.
Use KiCad 10 or later and retain the supplied project footprint library.

Keep insulated wire clear of component bodies, socket insertion paths,
antenna regions and enclosure hardware. Fit any links that would become
inaccessible before installing sockets/components. Check actual assembly
clearances; a copper DRC cannot validate three-dimensional wire placement.

Five capacitors have been added:
C9: 470uF 16V, Panasonic EEU-FR1C471, across LOCAL_5V/GND at the input.
    Body diameter 8 mm, body length 11.5 mm, lead pitch 3.5 mm.
C10/C12: 100uF 16V, Panasonic EEU-FR1C101, one per ESP after its
         power jumper. Body diameter 5 mm, length 11 mm, pitch 2 mm.
C11/C13: 100nF ceramic, one per ESP after its power jumper.
         Match the existing D5 mm, W2.5 mm, 5 mm lead-pitch envelope.
Electrolytic pad 1 is POSITIVE; pad 2 is NEGATIVE/GND. Check actual seated
height including tolerances against the enclosure.

C10/C11 connect to ESP_CTRL_5V (JP1 output), C12/C13 to ESP_AUDIO_5V
(JP2 output). Opening a power jumper isolates its ESP capacitors from
LOCAL_5V. No new capacitor connects local 5 V to Sony's separate supply.
These are decoupling/reservoir capacitors, not regulators or supply ORing.
The added capacitance increases startup/hot-plug inrush. Check the supply,
current limiting and dev-board USB/external-power paths before use.

GPIO assignments and original component placements are retained from v2.
All five buffer ENABLE requests remain ACTIVE HIGH, qualified by Sony
power in hardware. Initialize requests LOW; leave LOW during reset.
J12 remains sense-only: pin 1 common GND, pin 2 Sony 5 V.

The original v2 schematic issues remain for deliberate electrical review:
undriven abstract dev-board power/EN pins, pins without explicit
connections/no-connect marks, and dangling FLSTB/FLSDATA/FLSCLK labels.
Their intended J4 mapping was not invented or silently reassigned.

Review-before-fabrication is still required: actual connector pinouts,
passive values (including original generic-value capacitors), supply
sequencing, off-state back-powering/leakage, brownout behavior, RF antenna
clearances, fast-signal return paths and enclosure fit. This revision
has not been bench tested. A clean PCB DRC is not an electrical sign-off.
