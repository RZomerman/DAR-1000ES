DAR-1000ES v3 - physical insulated wire-link schedule

Install all 43 links. These are top-side WIRES, not top copper or vias.
All listed terminals of one JW reference must be electrically joined.
Coordinates below are mm, TOP VIEW, using the PCB coordinate origin.
Through-hole drill is 1.0 mm; choose wire and stripped leads to fit.
Use insulated AWG26 (or suitably sized equivalent) for logic signals.
Use insulated AWG22 or equivalent for LOCAL_5V, ESP 5 V and GND links;
verify current rating and fit. Leave room for component and enclosure clearances.
Lengths are drawn path lengths, not finished cut lengths; allow extra for
stripped ends, bends and routing clear of components. Do not copy bends
that would obstruct sockets, antennas or screws. Fit inaccessible links first.
For three/four-terminal links, use an insulated wire-tree assembly with
one lead into each hole; insulate any joined wire ends above the board.
