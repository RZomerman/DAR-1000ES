DAR-1000ES v2 - firmware and hardware interface changes

HARDWARE ENABLE CONTRACT
ENABLE_U2/U3/U4/U6/U7 are ACTIVE HIGH firmware requests.
Initialize every request LOW before configuring data outputs; leave LOW during boot/reset.
The AHCT00 NAND interlock asserts /OE LOW only when its request is HIGH AND SONY_POWER_GOOD is HIGH.
Do not use the former active-low direct-GPIO /OE logic.
J12 pin 1 = common GND, pin 2 = Sony 5 V SENSE ONLY.
Sony 5 V is never connected to LOCAL_5V. Grounds are intentionally common.
MCP100-475DI/TO: trip 4.50-4.75 V, release delay 150-700 ms.
The interlock uses TTL-compatible NAND ICs rather than five discrete transistor level shifters.
USB/external ESP power isolation/ORing is NOT supplied by this interlock; retain only verified power paths.

CONNECTOR PIN FUNCTIONS AND LABELS ARE UNCHANGED.
ESP symbol pin order is presentation-only in v2: read each printed GPIO name and physical header pin number.
Reserved PSRAM, USB, strapping, on-board LED and USB-UART GPIOs are excluded from newly assigned signals.

Straight-line signal crossing metric: 233 -> 101
Signal minimum-spanning-tree length metric: 2958.1 -> 2623.1 mm
These are optimization metrics, not final copper lengths or a proof of a global optimum.

Hardware timing, power sequencing and optical/audio operation require bench validation.
