USER DECISIONS (17 November 2026)
-------------------------------
V3 is rejected: its wire bridges are long, indirect and bend around the
board. Sony uses short, STRAIGHT VERTICAL bridges. Merely rotating a drawing
or replacing top copper with long wires does NOT satisfy this request.

The user explicitly selected 30 x 10 cm, NOT 21 x 19 cm. Increase the existing
210 mm long / 100 mm wide board to 300 mm long / 100 mm wide. With the
baseline coordinate orientation, extend the Y axis from 210 to 300 mm.
Move the cable connectors into that added 90 mm section. Keep their labels,
connector identities, cable pin order and electrical functions.

The user now ALLOWS moving components, unlike the earlier v2/v3 restriction.
Make generous, Sony-style through-hole assembly space. Redistribute the
ESP sockets, ICs, passives and jumpers to make a clean, practical layout.
Keep the controller/display/keypad section and audio section physically
separate, as they currently are. Keep the two connector groups distinct
within the extension too. Keep high-speed/audio wiring away from display
switching where practical. Respect RF antennas, USB access and enclosure
hardware. Preserve connector outward-facing horizontal JST-XH footprints.

All routed copper must be B.Cu. No F.Cu traces, no routing vias. Normal plated
through-hole pads with annuli on both faces ARE approved. Top-side wire
bridges are permitted ONLY as short, straight, north-south/Y-axis links:
exactly two terminals per bridge, identical world X coordinate at both
ends; no bends, diagonal links, horizontal links, long harnesses or multiway
wire trees. Prefer short standard pitches (e.g. 2.54-15.24 mm); this range
is a design target, not a user-specified hard numerical limit. Do not solve
congestion with a long vertical wire instead of improving placement.
Holes must be outside component bodies, sockets, insertion paths, screwhead
envelopes and silkscreen. Bridges can cross bottom tracks but not component
bodies or obstruct installed modules. Use the Sony photo as the style guide,
not as a measured mechanical drawing.

Preserve the five new power capacitors:
  C9: 470 uF, 16 V, Panasonic EEU-FR1C471, input LOCAL_5V to GND;
      D8 x 11.5 mm body, 3.5 mm lead pitch.
  C10/C12: 100 uF, 16 V, Panasonic EEU-FR1C101, one per ESP;
      D5 x 11 mm body, 2 mm pitch.
  C11/C13: 100 nF ceramic, one per ESP; agreed D5/W2.5/P5 mm envelope.
  C10/C11 are AFTER JP1 on ESP_CTRL_5V.
  C12/C13 are AFTER JP2 on ESP_AUDIO_5V.
  Electrolytic pin 1 positive; pin 2 GND. Place the 100 nF parts near supply
  pins and provide short supply/return connections, not merely visual proximity.

GPIO assignments, GPIO-to-SN74 inputs and equivalent SN74 gate/output
assignments may be optimized if that genuinely improves routing, as already
authorized earlier. Keep connector pin functions unchanged, synchronize
schematic/PCB, and document the new map against the v2/v3 map. Do not silently
change the firmware: current repository firmware may still use earlier maps.
Exclude octal-PSRAM, USB, strapping and board-specific LED/UART-conflict pins
unless independently verified safe. In particular reserve GPIO19/20 and
GPIO35/36/37; check both dev-board revisions' LED and USB-UART wiring.

ELECTRICAL INVARIANTS
--------------------
- Two Espressif ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1-N16R8 modules. Combined socket footprints
  represent 2 x 1x22 P2.54 sockets with 22.86 mm row spacing. Do not substitute
  a bare ESP module footprint or the incorrect "ESP3" name.
- TI SN74AHCT125N uses PDIP-14, 7.62 mm rows.
- Adafruit TCA8418 breakout product 4918 (NOT TCA4818) uses two 13-pin rows,
  2.54 mm pitch, 12.70 mm row spacing, 35.56 x 17.78 mm module envelope.
- Maintain the v2 Sony power hardware interlock. U8/U9 SN74AHCT00N and U10
  MCP100-475 qualify all five buffer banks:
      /OE = NAND(GPIO_ENABLE, SONY_POWER_GOOD).
  Firmware ENABLE requests are ACTIVE HIGH, default LOW via 100k pulldowns.
  OE pull-ups remain on local 5 V; never directly put those 5 V pull-ups
  onto ESP GPIOs. Keep Sony power sense separate from local 5 V.
- J2 pin 1 GND, pin 2 LOCAL_5V.
  JP1/JP2 pin 1 LOCAL_5V; pin 2 respective post-jumper ESP supply.
  J12 pin 1 common GND, pin 2 SONY_5V_SENSE ONLY, never a power feed.
- Source ERC has 63 review items (47 pin_not_connected, 10 power_pin_not_driven,
  2 pin_not_driven, 3 label_dangling, 1 same_local_global_label). Do not waive
  or misrepresent these as electrical sign-off. FLSTB/FLSDATA/FLSCLK/J4 mapping
  is unresolved; do not invent connector routing to make a report green.
  Preserve intended original circuit behavior. Flag genuine electrical
  ambiguities explicitly instead of guessing dangerous Sony-side mappings.
- Existing minimum clearance is 0.20 mm, signal width 0.30 mm, power 0.80 mm;
  min width 0.25 mm. More hand-soldering clearance is desirable where space
  permits, but the existing TO-92 pads have only about 0.22 mm copper gap.
- Keep six original mounting-hole locations if practical; the extension may
  need additional mounting support. Document changes and do not claim Sony
  chassis compatibility without dimensions/physical fit verification.

IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE / KNOWN PITFALLS
--------------------------------------
Use KiCad 10 (source file format version 20260206), its footprint/symbol
libraries, kicad-cli and pcbnew Python on Linux. KiCad 8/9 may not read these
files correctly. Provision suitable tooling on the runner before editing.
Keep all paths relocatable; supplied library tables already use KIPRJMOD
and KICAD10_FOOTPRINT_DIR. Do not downgrade format or fake successful checks.

Plan placement and routing together. Use a single-bottom-layer routing model
with deliberately placed short vertical jumper components, or an equivalent
constrained algorithm. Freerouting's preferred_direction=vertical is a COST,
not a hard direction constraint. Do not rerun an unconstrained two-layer
autorouter and convert its arbitrary F.Cu paths to long bent wire harnesses.

The earlier working autorouter was official Freerouting 2.5.0 Java JAR:
https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting/releases/tag/v2.5.0
The downloaded freerouting-2.5.0.jar had SHA256
f6f51bb02245e8e717f9359bd260cc9c5c0b1bc0acc8b7cb2cd5b8ffeb5de3c7.
Use a compatible JRE (Temurin 25 worked); obtain Linux builds, not the
earlier Windows JRE. Native Freerouting builds failed due to missing GraalVM
serialization constructors (ComponentOutline, ObstacleArea); the JAR worked.
Disable diagnostics, API server and GUI in every router process:
  FREEROUTING__USAGE_AND_DIAGNOSTIC_DATA__DISABLE_ANALYTICS=true
  FREEROUTING__API_SERVER__ENABLED=false
  FREEROUTING__GUI__ENABLED=false
Bound heap/workers (previously -Xmx2g, -mt 2).
--router.automatic_neckdown=false avoids undersize routing, but inspect
fanout too: it independently generated two 0.225 mm sections in v3.
Match the 0.50 mm copper-to-board-edge constraint.

Do NOT parse/re-emit entire Specctra DSNs with a generic KiCad S-expression
emitter: quoted pin references have adjacent quoted/unquoted tokens and get
silently corrupted. Make surgical text edits or use a genuine DSN parser.
pcbnew.ImportSpecctraSES works, but import into a fresh untracked board so
tracks do not accumulate. Repeated pcbnew.Remove(footprint) operations caused
SWIG ownership corruption; AST editing plus fresh processes was reliable.

Physical installed bridges can be represented accurately with two same-net,
same-number through-hole pads and KiCad 10
  (duplicate_pad_numbers_are_jumpers yes).
This property was tested: turning it off creates a missing connection;
turning it on models the INSTALLED WIRE. Do not use it to fabricate internal
connectivity where no actual two-terminal straight bridge will be fitted.
Provide project-local footprints, clear reference labels and a wire schedule.
Distinguish soldered wire bridges from removable JP1/JP2 shunts.
Temporary router-only via keepouts were exported with pcbnew rule areas:
  SetIsRuleArea(True), SetDoNotAllowVias(True),
  SetDoNotAllowTracks(False), SetDoNotAllowPads(False),
  SetDoNotAllowZoneFills(False), SetDoNotAllowFootprints(False).
Keepout bodies must include component courtyards, visible reference/value
text and screwhead clearance. Temporary planning vias must all be removed
from the final design and replaced by actual jumper holes when appropriate.
Include existing jumper holes in clearance calculations for later jumpers.
